01 — Architecture

Dumb terminals, smart backend

Edge devices read a card and send a raw event. All rules — who the card belongs to, check-in vs check-out, deduplication — live in the backend where they can be audited, changed, and tested without touching hardware.

02 — Integrity

Append-only audit trail

Every scan is written once and never mutated. Corrections are new records with an actor and a reason. You have a complete, tamper-evident attendance history from the very first scan.

03 — Privacy

Credentials stored as fingerprints

Raw card UIDs are never saved. The system stores only an HMAC fingerprint derived from a rotating secret. A stolen database cannot reveal which card belongs to which person.

04 — Ownership

On-prem, no cloud required

Deploy from a single Docker image. No external accounts, no subscription, no data leaving the building. Runs on an air-gapped network. Your data. Your hardware. Your rules.

Built on honest engineering

  • The terminal is a dumb edge device. It reads hardware, timestamps, and sends. Every business rule lives in the backend — that is where decisions belong, where they can be tested, and where they leave a trace.
  • An event is permanent the moment it is written. The attendance ledger is append-only. This is not a restriction — it is a guarantee. You can always reconstruct exactly what happened and why.
  • Credentials are identifiers, not secrets. NFC cards can be cloned. So we never store the raw UID — only an HMAC fingerprint keyed to a secret you control. The fingerprint is useless without the key.
  • Your data does not leave the building. CrispyClocks is designed for on-premise deployment from day one. GDPR compliance is an architectural property, not a checkbox.
